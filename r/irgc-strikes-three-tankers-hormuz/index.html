Tanker ablaze near Hormuz after Revolutionary Guards say they hit three ships

09 Nov 2026, 15:10 GMT · London
Iran's Revolutionary Guards say they attacked three tankers on Sunday for using routes it had not approved in coordination with the US military.

An oil tanker was seen burning close to the Strait of Hormuz after Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said it had attacked three tankers on Sunday.

The Guards accused the vessels of taking what they called unauthorised routes through the waterway while working with the American military, which has been coordinating a shuttle system to keep some oil moving through the strait during the war.

Tehran has repeatedly warned ships that leaving the corridors it designates could expose them to attack. Iranian state television said on Monday that 11 tankers had been targeted over the previous ten days, and that four of them were unable to carry on with their voyages. Media close to the Guards also claimed that navigation systems in the southern part of the strait were hit overnight, halting tankers that were due to pass under the American arrangement.

The latest incidents add to the strain on one of the world's most important energy routes, through which a large share of the Gulf's oil and gas exports normally travels, and come as mediators try to stop the conflict between Washington and Tehran from returning to full scale fighting.
