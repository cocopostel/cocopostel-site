US troops in Germany await their fate as Pentagon weighs Europe drawdown

10 Nov 2026, 11:01 GMT · London
An American Army unit stationed in Germany does not know what comes next while defence chiefs consider a large cut in forces across the continent.

American soldiers based in Germany are living with uncertainty as the Pentagon studies a major reduction in the number of US forces stationed in Europe.

One Army unit in the country says it does not know what its future holds while the review continues. No final decision on the scale or timing of any cut has been announced.

Germany hosts the largest American military presence in Europe, and the bases there have for decades served as a hub for operations across the continent, the Middle East and Africa. Any significant withdrawal would be felt by allies who rely on the American presence as a deterrent, and by German communities whose economies are tied to the bases.

The review comes at a sensitive moment. Washington is leading fresh talks between Russia and Ukraine, and European governments are already uneasy about American proposals that touch on the future security order on the continent. Officials in Europe are likely to press for clarity before any changes are made.
