Storm floods Gaza tent camps, killing four members of one family

10 Nov 2026, 19:24 GMT · London
Thousands of displaced Palestinians lost their shelters as heavy weather swept the crowded camps, while Israel says there will be no rebuilding until Hamas disarms.

A storm that tore through the crowded tent camps of Gaza has killed at least four people, all from a single family, and left thousands of displaced Palestinians without shelter.

The camps, home to families forced from their houses during the war, offer little protection against heavy rain and wind. Flooding swept through flimsy tents and makeshift structures, leaving many people exposed to the cold with nowhere else to go.

The deaths add to growing alarm over conditions in the territory as winter approaches. Much of Gaza's housing and infrastructure lies in ruins, and aid groups have repeatedly warned that the displaced are poorly equipped for bad weather.

Israel has made clear that large scale reconstruction will not begin until Hamas gives up its weapons, a position that leaves families facing the season in temporary shelter. The floods have also drawn criticism of the relief effort from the Board of Peace, which has said Israel's response falls short.
