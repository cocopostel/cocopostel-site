Analysts doubt American business offers will soften Putin over Ukraine

10 Nov 2026, 10:04 GMT · London
As talks open in Abu Dhabi, experts say the Russian president has shown he will pay a heavy price for his aims and sees little reason to stop fighting.

Offers of business deals from Washington are unlikely to persuade President Vladimir Putin to give up ground in Ukraine, according to analysts watching the latest round of peace talks.

The negotiations, led by the United States, are taking place in Abu Dhabi this week, with delegations from Washington, Moscow and Kyiv present. Part of the American approach has been to hold out the prospect of economic cooperation with Russia once the war ends.

Analysts argue that this may carry little weight in the Kremlin. They say Mr Putin has already shown he is willing to absorb very heavy costs, military and economic, in pursuit of his goals in Ukraine, and that from his point of view there is not much reason to halt the fighting now.

That assessment suggests the gap between the two sides on territory and security guarantees remains wide, even as officials speak of some flexibility. A ceasefire is not expected to be on the agenda this week, and European governments are watching closely for any concessions that could affect their own security.
