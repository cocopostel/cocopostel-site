Debris from intercepted Houthi missile damages Aramco tower in Riyadh

08 Nov 2026, 13:05 GMT · London
Saudi officials say a building in the capital's financial district was struck by falling fragments after a missile from Yemen was shot down, with no injuries reported.

A tower belonging to Saudi Aramco in the financial district of Riyadh was damaged on Sunday after Yemen's Houthi movement fired missiles towards the Saudi capital.

Saudi authorities said the building was hit by fragments that fell after air defences intercepted an incoming missile. They reported that nobody was hurt.

The incident brings the Yemen conflict closer to the heart of the kingdom's economy. The state oil giant is the country's most valuable company, and the district where its tower stands is home to banks, ministries and corporate offices.

It comes as fighting between the Houthis and Yemen's Saudi backed government continues to escalate, with a sharp rise in strikes and casualties in recent days. The Houthis have repeatedly launched missiles and drones at Saudi territory, while the Saudi led coalition has carried out air raids on areas the group controls.

Officials gave no estimate of the cost of the damage, and there was no immediate word on how many missiles were fired in the latest attack.
