Thomas Markle returned to federal custody in Florida after monitor allegedly removed

11 Nov 2026, 02:41 GMT · London
A magistrate in Tampa refused bond for the 82 year old father of the Duchess of Sussex, ruling he broke the terms of his release while awaiting trial on a child abuse images charge he denies.

Thomas Markle, father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, is back in federal custody in Florida after prosecutors said he took off the electronic monitor he had been ordered to wear.

Mr Markle, 82, appeared before a federal magistrate in Tampa on Tuesday and was refused bond. Prosecutors told the court he removed the device early that morning. The judge found he had breached the terms of his pretrial release and, pointing to the lack of suitable housing, concluded that no set of conditions could guarantee public safety or his return to court. A medical assessment was also ordered.

He was arrested last week and placed under house detention after FBI agents searched storage units in Los Angeles that he had rented since 2008. Officials say about 78 items were seized, including negatives, slides and books they allege contain child sexual abuse material. He faces a single count of possession and has called the accusations lies.

The federal prosecutor in central California said release conditions were orders, not suggestions, and vowed to pursue the case fully.

Mr Markle moved to the Philippines last year after several strokes and later returned to Florida, where he was reported to be recovering from a leg amputation. The duchess has been estranged from him since 2018 and has said she does not allow him contact with her children.
