Anthropic aims for a $1.3tn valuation as it prepares to list in New York

10 Nov 2026, 14:46 GMT · London
The maker of the Claude chatbot will price its shares between $70 and $78 and hopes to raise up to $64bn, below the figure some backers had predicted.

Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company behind the Claude chatbot, could be worth up to about $1.3tn when its shares begin trading on the Nasdaq exchange in New York later this month.

The company said it would sell its shares at between $70 and $78 each, raising as much as $64bn. That would put its value somewhere between $1.1tn and $1.3tn, a vast sum but short of the $2tn that some of its investors had talked of during the summer.

The listing comes at a nervous moment for the AI industry. Technology shares fell steeply after last week's midterm elections in the United States, amid worries that a Congress led by Democrats could slow the growth of AI and the construction of data centres. Broader concerns persist about the enormous amounts being spent on the technology and whether that investment will pay off.

Anthropic still loses money, but its revenue has grown at a remarkable pace. In the six months to the end of June it brought in $16.2bn, up from $1.1bn in the same period a year earlier.

One market analyst said the size of the fundraising was fairly bold given recent turbulence, and suggested it showed confidence that demand for AI would hold up.

At its top estimate Anthropic would still be valued below SpaceX, which listed in June at close to $1.8tn. Anthropic was founded only five years ago, in 2021, by the siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei and five colleagues, all of whom had previously worked at OpenAI, the developer of ChatGPT.
