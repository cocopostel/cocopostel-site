Protesters throw milk at Joe Biden in Dublin over his backing for Israel

11 Nov 2026, 02:12 GMT · London
The former president was at Trinity College on an unannounced visit to see his son Hunter receive an award when pro Palestinian demonstrators targeted him.

Pro Palestinian protesters threw milk towards Joe Biden during a visit to Dublin, accusing the former US president of standing behind Israel during the war in Gaza.

Mr Biden had made an unannounced trip to Trinity College in the Irish capital, where his son Hunter was being honoured with an award for public discourse. The demonstrators confronted him at the event.

The incident underlines how strongly feeling over Gaza continues to follow American political figures abroad, long after Mr Biden left the White House. Ireland has been one of the most vocal European critics of Israel's conduct in the war, and Gaza remains a cause that draws large crowds there.

Mr Biden, who has Irish roots and has visited the country often, recently said that his cancer treatment would limit the promotion of his new memoir. There was no immediate word on whether anyone was detained.
