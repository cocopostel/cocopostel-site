Indonesia names fourth suspect in alleged presidential wiretap scandal

06 Nov 2026, 09:00 GMT · London
Police identified a senior Criminal Investigation Agency official as the latest figure linked to claimed interception of President Prabowo Subianto's communications, widening a rift with predecessor Joko Widodo.

Indonesian police have named a fourth suspect in a widening scandal over the alleged wiretapping of President Prabowo Subianto.

At a late Thursday news conference officers identified a senior law enforcement official known by the initials SU as the newest suspect. Three officials were arrested earlier in the week. Police said SU served as monitoring chief at the Criminal Investigation Agency between 2010 and 2019. Local media later named him as Slamet Uliandi. Investigators allege that as head of the agency's information and technology unit from 2021 to 2026 he authorised the wiretapping and later approved deletion of interception records. He has not yet been detained because of a health condition, police said.

Among those already arrested is former national police chief and Prabowo's chief of staff Listyo Sigit Prabowo, widely seen as a loyalist of predecessor Joko Widodo. The case has exposed a deepening rift between the current president and the former ally who helped deliver his landslide 2024 win. Widodo's son, Gibran Rakabuming Raka, serves as Prabowo's vice president; lawmakers are now weighing a probe into Raka's eligibility that could be a first step toward removing him.

Police say they are still investigating motives and have questioned 13 officers plus five criminal and IT experts. A special team has been formed. Jakarta stayed largely calm even as thousands of troops deployed after the revelations, with military tents and buses visible and only a small number of soldiers on the streets.

The scandal piles pressure on Prabowo amid public discontent over costly populist policies and weakening investor confidence in Indonesia's roughly $1.5 trillion economy. What began as a cybersecurity inquiry is now a political stress test of the alliance that brought him to power.
