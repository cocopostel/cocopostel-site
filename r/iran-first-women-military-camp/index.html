Iran opens its first all women military training camp as it braces for new US strikes

11 Nov 2026, 03:21 GMT · London
Dozens of women volunteers took part in drills in central Tehran as the country prepares for the possibility of renewed American attacks.

Iran has opened what it describes as its first military training camp for women only, a sign of how seriously Tehran is preparing for the possibility of fresh American strikes.

Dozens of women took part in exercises at Azadi Square in the capital. They belong to a volunteer movement whose members say they are ready to give their lives in defence of the country.

The camp is the latest step in a wider mobilisation at home since the war with the United States began. Officials have tightened internal security in recent weeks while insisting the country will not bow to pressure, even as Washington alternates between threats of force and talk of a settlement.

The public display in one of Tehran's best known squares appears designed as much for an audience abroad as for Iranians themselves, signalling that the authorities intend to draw on every part of society should fighting resume. No details were given on how long the training would last or how many women are expected to join future rounds.
