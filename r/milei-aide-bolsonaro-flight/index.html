Milei aide reportedly met Bolsonaro as he fled Brazil

05 Nov 2026, 16:06 GMT · London
A senior adviser to Argentina's president greeted defeated candidate Flavio Bolsonaro at an Argentine airport, according to people involved. The episode raises fresh questions about Buenos Aires' role in his departure.

A senior adviser to Argentina's president Javier Milei reportedly met Flavio Bolsonaro at an Argentine airport as the defeated Brazilian presidential candidate left Brazil, according to people involved in the episode. The account places Buenos Aires closer to the departure than a purely passive neighbour.

Flavio Bolsonaro's exit after a lost election was already politically charged at home. The reported airport greeting by a Milei aide sharpens questions about how much help, if any, Argentine officials gave and whether it was personal, informal or coordinated. Those involved have described a meeting on arrival; they have not framed it as a formal asylum process in public summaries available so far.

The story matters for regional politics. Brazil and Argentina are major neighbours whose leaders often trade barbs across the border. Any perception that Argentine power circles eased a Brazilian rival's flight will feed opposition attacks in Brasilia and awkward questions in Buenos Aires.

For now the public record rests on those accounts of an airport meeting. Investigators and journalists will press for passenger logs, messages and official statements. Until those appear, the core fact on offer is limited but pointed: a Milei adviser was there when Bolsonaro arrived.
