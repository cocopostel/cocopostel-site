Scalise and Jordan renew old rivalry in race to lead shrunken House Republicans

07 Nov 2026, 17:32 GMT · London
The current majority leader and the Judiciary Committee chair are both running to become minority leader after the party lost the House.

Two veteran Republicans are competing to lead their party in the House after it lost its majority in the midterm elections.

Steve Scalise of Louisiana, the current House majority leader, and Jim Jordan of Ohio, who chairs the Judiciary Committee, are both seeking the post of minority leader. The contest is a rematch between longtime rivals who have clashed for the top jobs before.

Whoever wins will take charge of a much smaller conference that must adjust to life in opposition, with Democrats set to control the chamber and the Senate from January.

The new minority leader will face the task of rebuilding after a heavy defeat, while managing relations with a White House that is now facing a hostile Congress.
