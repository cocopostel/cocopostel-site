Justice Department prepares for wave of oversight as Democrats take Congress

08 Nov 2026, 11:00 GMT · London
Democrats have vowed to examine how the department has been run during Trump's second term, while the president has told it to investigate them.

The US Justice Department is bracing for intense scrutiny from Capitol Hill after Democrats swept last week's midterm elections and won control of Congress.

Democratic lawmakers have promised to look closely at the department's actions during President Donald Trump's second term, including decisions they see as politically driven. Control of committees will give them the power to call officials to testify, demand documents and hold public hearings.

The prospect sets up a clash between the two branches. Trump has already ordered the department to open inquiries into Democrats, a move his critics describe as an attempt to turn law enforcement against political opponents.

Officials inside the department are expected to face a heavy load of requests and hearings in the months ahead, as the new majority makes oversight of federal law enforcement one of its early priorities.
