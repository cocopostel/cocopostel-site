Washington snubs UN review of its human rights record for second year

10 Nov 2026, 18:29 GMT · London
No American delegation turned up in Geneva, forcing the Human Rights Council to postpone the examination until January as rights groups warned of surging abuses.

The United States has boycotted a United Nations review of its human rights record for the second year running, leaving an empty seat in Geneva and prompting criticism from rights groups.

Every UN member state goes through the review every four to five years. When the session opened on Tuesday, the president of the Human Rights Council, Indonesia's Sidharto Reza Suryodipuro, told the chamber that no American delegation was present. The council then put off its examination of the US until January.

The Trump administration pulled out of the council in February 2025, shortly after the start of the president's second term, arguing it was biased against Israel. It has since withdrawn from several other UN bodies, cut funding and launched the war on Iran without seeking UN backing.

A State Department spokesperson said taking part would amount to endorsing the council, which the official dismissed as an irredeemable body hostile to America. The spokesperson did not address the substance of concerns about the US record.

The council has not formally criticised Washington, but experts have raised alarm over issues such as mass deportations, which the administration says comply with US law. Documents show governments had planned to question the US on abortion access and the death penalty, and almost 200 submissions from American and international groups covered subjects from migration to racial discrimination.

A civil liberties campaigner in Geneva said attacks on basic rights in America were unmatched in generations. A former senior council official warned that when the world's leading power fails to appear without even explaining why, the credibility of the whole review system suffers.

During his first term, the president did submit the required US report to the review.
