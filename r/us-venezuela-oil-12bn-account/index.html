Washington has taken in $12bn from Venezuelan oil, report reveals

10 Nov 2026, 16:23 GMT · London
A report on a bank account controlled by the United States offers a first look at how American officials handle Venezuela's oil money after the capture of its president.

The United States has received about $12bn from the sale of Venezuelan oil, according to a newly published report on the workings of a bank account under American control.

The account sits at the centre of the arrangement Washington put in place to handle Venezuelan revenue after American forces seized the country's president earlier this year. Until now, little had been disclosed about how much money was moving through it or how it was being managed.

The report offers the first detailed look at that system and at the scale of the oil income now passing through American hands. It arrives as the operation in Venezuela faces growing scrutiny at home, with the Republican congressman Thomas Massie this week citing the raid on Caracas in articles of impeachment against Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth and arguing that the goal was control of the country's oil.

How the money has been spent, and who decides its use, was not immediately clear from the public details of the report.
