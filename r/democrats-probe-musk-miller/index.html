Democrats prepare inquiries into Musk, Miller and the Trump family

08 Nov 2026, 17:58 GMT · London
Incoming committee chairs say they want answers on mass federal firings, the immigration crackdown and the business affairs of the president's relatives.

Democrats moving into power in Congress have begun to name the people they most want to scrutinise once they take the gavel in January, with Elon Musk and Stephen Miller high on the list.

Robert Garcia, a Democrat from California who is in line to chair the House oversight committee, said on Sunday that inquiries would look at Mr Musk's role in the mass dismissal of federal workers last year through the Department of Government Efficiency, the cost cutting body he helped to lead. Mr Garcia said there was wide interest across Congress in speaking to the world's richest man. The same committee is also expected to examine Mr Miller's part in President Donald Trump's immigration crackdown. Mr Miller is due to start work as White House chief of staff on Monday.

Jamie Raskin, who is poised to take the chair of the House judiciary committee, has asked for information on the business dealings of Jared Kushner, the president's son in law, and of Donald Trump Jr.

The planned investigations come after Mr Trump threatened Democrats who intend to oversee his administration and his family, and said he had told the Justice Department to look into them. Attorney General Todd Blanche said on Sunday that investigations were possible and claimed Republicans had faced dangerous threats, without saying what offences he believed had been committed.

The clash sets up a bitter two years in Washington, with each side promising to use the powers of office against the other after a midterm vote that handed Democrats control of both chambers.
