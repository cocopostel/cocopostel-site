Eight killed in a string of shootings in western Turkey

05 Nov 2026, 19:32 GMT · London
Eight people died and one was seriously hurt after shootings across rural Afyonkarahisar province. Police later arrested a suspect in neighbouring Eskisehir after a manhunt.

Eight people have been killed and one seriously injured in a series of shootings in western Turkey's Afyonkarahisar province, local officials and media say.

Victims were found on Thursday in several places and in three separate vehicles in rural areas northeast of the provincial capital. State run reporting said three people died near Ayazini village and three near Kiyir, while two women found injured in Yukaritandir later died in hospital. Two teachers were among the dead, according to local accounts.

The suspect fled, prompting a large police operation and road closures into the affected villages. Authorities later said he had been arrested in Eskisehir province. Turkish media published CCTV images of a man in a black jacket, white trousers and glasses holding a long weapon beside a red minivan. A vehicle allegedly taken by the attacker was later found stuck in mud after he abandoned it and continued on foot, the newspaper Milliyet reported.

The Afyonkarahisar governorate said all relevant state institutions had been working in coordination from the first moments of the case. No broader motive had been set out in the early public statements summarised in international coverage.

The killings shake a quiet rural belt rather than a major city, which is why the manhunt and the CCTV stills spread quickly through Turkish news. Families of the victims, including those of the two teachers, now wait for prosecutors to explain why the gunman moved between villages and whether anyone else was involved.
