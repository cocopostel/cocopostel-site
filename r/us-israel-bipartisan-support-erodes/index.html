Support for Israel in Washington frays in both parties

09 Nov 2026, 10:02 GMT · London
Republicans including the vice president are calling for change, while many Democrats push to restrict military aid, ending a long era of near automatic backing.

The broad agreement across American politics on support for Israel is weakening, with voices in both major parties now pressing for new limits on the relationship.

On the Republican side, Vice President JD Vance is among those who have called for a change of course. Among Democrats, a large number of lawmakers have been leading a campaign to place restrictions on the military aid that Washington sends to Israel.

For decades, backing for Israel was one of the few issues on which leaders of both parties reliably agreed. That consensus now looks far less secure than at any point in recent memory.

The results of last week's midterm elections, which handed Democrats more power in Congress, are likely to sharpen the debate. Any attempt to attach conditions to aid would mark a significant break with past practice and could become a defining fight of the new session.
