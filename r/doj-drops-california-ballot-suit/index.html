Justice Department drops its bid to set aside 20,000 California ballots

07 Nov 2026, 04:09 GMT · London
The Trump administration has abandoned a lawsuit over thousands of mail in votes after the Supreme Court refused to back it before polling day.

The US Justice Department has dismissed its lawsuit against California over roughly 20,000 mail in ballots, ending a high profile legal fight over the state's midterm vote count.

The Trump administration had sought to have the ballots set aside, but the effort suffered a decisive setback before election day when the Supreme Court rejected it. With that ruling in place and the votes already cast and counted, the department has now withdrawn the case.

The suit was part of a wider push by the administration to challenge voting procedures in states run by Democrats, particularly around postal voting, which President Donald Trump has repeatedly attacked as open to fraud without producing evidence of widespread abuse.

The dismissal comes as the administration weighs its response to a midterm result that handed Democrats control of Congress.
