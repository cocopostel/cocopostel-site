American tankers and other aircraft suddenly leave a second British base

10 Nov 2026, 11:35 GMT · London
At least a dozen US refuelling planes and other aircraft departed on Monday from a major Air Force hub in Britain, with no reason yet given.

At least a dozen American refuelling aircraft, together with other planes, left a British base on Monday in a departure that has not been explained.

The base is one of the most important hubs for the US Air Force in Britain, and it is the second British site this has happened at. Neither Washington nor London has said publicly why the aircraft were moved or where they were sent.

Tanker aircraft keep fighters and bombers in the air over long distances, so a sudden move of this kind is usually watched closely as a possible sign of shifting military plans. It comes as the Pentagon reviews how many troops it keeps in Europe, and as the United States weighs its posture towards Iran after President Donald Trump said further strikes were unlikely while keeping forces ready.

Until officials give an explanation, the reasons for the move remain unclear, and it is not known whether the aircraft will return.
