Six killed in New York building fire

05 Nov 2026, 15:00 GMT · London
A blaze in a New York City building has left six people dead and others hurt. Around 230 firefighters and medics responded, and the cause is still being investigated.

Six people have died in a major fire at a residential building in Brooklyn, New York City, officials say, with several others hurt. The blaze was reported around 01:40 local time on Thursday, which is 06:40 GMT, and more than 230 firefighters and emergency medics were sent to the scene near Sunset Park.

Crews found the first and second floors already engulfed when they arrived. Four people were pronounced dead at the scene. A man and a woman later died in hospital. Six other civilians were injured, two of them critically, and three firefighters were also hurt. Mayor Zohran Mamdani said he was heartbroken by the deaths and asked New Yorkers to pray for those still recovering.

The cause remains under investigation. A hazardous materials team attended because a lithium ion battery was present, though officials have not said whether it started the fire. That detail will matter for any later safety advice, but investigators have not yet linked it to the ignition.

For now the focus is on the victims, the injured and the families. Fire officials will piece together how the flames moved so quickly through the lower floors, and whether alarms, exits or building systems played any part. Until that work is done, the public facts are the death toll, the scale of the response, and an open inquiry.
