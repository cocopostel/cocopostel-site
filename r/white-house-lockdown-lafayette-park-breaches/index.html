White House briefly locked down after car crash and barrier jump at Lafayette Park

09 Nov 2026, 18:57 GMT · London
Two people were detained on Monday morning as the Secret Service dealt with a smoking car on the pavement and a separate intruder, with the president inside the residence.

The White House was briefly sealed off on Monday after two separate security incidents in Lafayette Park, the square directly opposite the presidential residence, the Secret Service has said.

At around 10am local time, or 15:00 GMT, a driver mounted the pavement on the northern edge of the park near the junction of 16th and H Streets. Secret Service police and National Guard units rushed to the scene. Footage showed a silver saloon pouring out smoke as officers surrounded it, and the driver climbing out and lashing out at them before being arrested. The agency said the man had been combative but was quickly detained. A police bomb disposal team was then brought in to check the vehicle.

At roughly the same moment, a second person climbed over a security barrier at the park's northwest corner and was also taken into custody. Officials have not said whether the two events were linked.

President Donald Trump was inside the executive residence at the time. The building was placed under lockdown and nearby roads were closed, with restrictions lifted about an hour later, according to reporters travelling with the president.

Authorities have not disclosed what charges either person may face, and both incidents remain under investigation.
