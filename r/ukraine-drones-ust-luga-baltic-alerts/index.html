Ukrainian drones set Ust Luga ablaze as Baltic states sound shelter alerts

11 Nov 2026, 04:34 GMT · London
Strikes on Russia's northwest and Russian attacks on Ukrainian power sites continued as US brokered peace talks entered their final day, with Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia warning of stray drones.

Russia and Ukraine kept up their long range attacks overnight even as US mediated peace talks between the two sides moved into their second and final day.

Ukrainian drones struck Russia's northwest, a region home to major Baltic Sea export terminals and a large oil refinery, starting a fire in the area of the port of Ust Luga and injuring three people. In the Smolensk region near Belarus, falling drone debris killed two people, the local governor said. A day earlier the governor of the Leningrad region said 146 drones had been shot down there, after a Ukrainian strike on Monday hit the Kirishi refinery, Russia's second largest, where local officials said a woman was killed.

Ukraine also came under fire. A Russian strike cut electricity in parts of Kryvyi Rih, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's home city, while one person was hurt in Kharkiv and six in Zaporizhzhia. Russia's defence ministry said its drones hit three power substations in the Kryvyi Rih and Odesa regions and that it had struck a cargo ship southwest of Odesa that it said was carrying weapons for Ukraine. Russian officials also said Ukrainian shelling wounded civilians in the occupied Donetsk region, mostly around Horlivka.

The fighting spilled over into alarm along NATO's eastern edge. Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia issued alerts urging people to take shelter from unidentified drones entering from Russia or Belarus, with the Lithuanian warning covering Kaunas, a city of about 300,000. NATO air defence jets were scrambled and no injuries were reported. Finland briefly imposed air and sea restrictions in the eastern Gulf of Finland as a precaution before lifting them.

Zelenskyy described the first day of the three way talks in the Gulf as constructive and substantive, but the overnight barrage showed how far the battlefield remains from any pause.
