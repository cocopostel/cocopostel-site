Kenya cult leader gets 200 years for murdering 191 people

06 Nov 2026, 12:19 GMT · London
Paul Mackenzie is sentenced in Mombasa for ordering followers to starve children to death in Shakahola forest so they could meet Jesus.

A Kenyan court has sentenced doomsday cult leader Paul Mackenzie to 200 years in prison for the murder of 191 people, all but one of them children. Justice Diana Mochache said he bore primary blame for telling followers to fast until death in one of the country's deadliest cases of religious extremism.

Mackenzie and 27 others were convicted after instructing members to starve their children so they could meet Jesus. Prosecutors in Mombasa had sought the death penalty, unused in Kenya since 1987, and cited what they called his lack of remorse. The judge said the term must send a clear signal that such crimes draw the heaviest penalties the law allows.

Police arrested Mackenzie in 2023 after more than 400 bodies were found in shallow graves in Shakahola forest near the coast. The inquiry stunned Kenya and drew global attention. Before Friday's hearing, a relative who lost eight family members including two grandchildren said he wanted a sentence that matched the harm done.

Mackenzie and his co accused asked for mercy. At trial they had pleaded not guilty and insisted nobody was forced to die. In her lengthy judgment last month the judge said followers treated him as Jesus's mouthpiece. An appeal path remains open under Kenyan law.
