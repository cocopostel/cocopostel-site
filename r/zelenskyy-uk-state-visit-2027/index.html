Zelenskyy set for UK state visit in 2027

07 Nov 2026, 05:00 GMT · London
Britain plans a grand ceremonial welcome marking five years since Russia’s full scale invasion of Ukraine.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy is to receive a United Kingdom state visit in 2027, a ceremonial honour planned to mark five years since Russia’s full scale invasion.

State visits are among the highest formal welcomes Britain extends to foreign leaders, typically pairing palace pageantry with substantive talks. Scheduling the occasion for the fifth anniversary year underscores how central Ukraine remains to British foreign and defence policy even as United States politics shift after the midterms.

The announcement lands while Russian strikes continue against Ukrainian cities and as Washington hosts Russian envoys for talks that have excluded Kyiv from some sessions. British officials have repeatedly argued that military aid, training and long term security commitments must continue regardless of fluctuations in American congressional maths.

A 2027 date gives planners time to arrange the full ceremonial programme and related bilateral meetings. It also offers Kyiv a high visibility platform in London at a moment when European allies are debating post war guarantees, reconstruction finance and how to deter another Russian offensive.

For Zelenskyy the visit would showcase enduring G7 support after years of wartime diplomacy. For Britain it signals that Ukraine policy remains a multi year national commitment rather than a short emergency response. The war’s fifth anniversary framing ties the pomp to a solemn milestone rather than a victory parade, reflecting a conflict that still lacks a settled endgame.
