Jeffries says he would firmly back impeaching Pete Hegseth

08 Nov 2026, 15:51 GMT · London
The Democrat expected to become House speaker says he will discuss the question with colleagues on Tuesday.

Hakeem Jeffries, the Democrat widely expected to take the gavel as speaker when the new House sits in January, has said he would be a firm yes on impeaching Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth.

Mr Jeffries said he planned to talk the matter over with fellow Democratic lawmakers on Tuesday, suggesting no formal decision has yet been made by the party.

The remark is one of the clearest signs yet of how Democrats intend to use the power they won in last week's midterm elections, which handed them control of both chambers of Congress. Party leaders have already signalled an appetite for aggressive oversight of the Trump administration.

Mr Hegseth has been one of the most contested figures in the cabinet. The White House has recently denied reports that he is preparing to leave his post.

An impeachment in the House would require only a simple majority, but removal from office would need a two thirds vote in the Senate, a bar that remains far out of reach.
