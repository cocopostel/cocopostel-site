Washington orders Lebanon's consul general in Detroit to leave

09 Nov 2026, 10:35 GMT · London
The secretary of state acted after old social media posts resurfaced that appeared to praise the 1983 bombing of the Marine barracks in Beirut.

The United States has ordered Lebanon's consul general in Detroit to leave the country, after social media posts attributed to him resurfaced and drew outrage in Washington.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio told Ibrahim Charara to depart over posts that allegedly praised the 1983 attack on the Marine barracks in Beirut. The 1983 bombing, which Washington blames on Hezbollah, killed 241 American service members.

The expulsion of a serving diplomat is a rare step and is likely to add strain to relations between Washington and Beirut at a delicate time for Lebanon, where the armed group remains a powerful force.

Detroit and the surrounding area are home to one of the largest Lebanese communities in America, making the consulate an important link for families on both sides of the Atlantic.
