Senator Ron Johnson accuses Trump aides of serving special interests

07 Nov 2026, 22:30 GMT · London
The Wisconsin Republican used a popular podcast appearance to allege corruption inside the administration, citing claims by a former White House adviser.

Ron Johnson, the Republican senator from Wisconsin and a longtime ally of President Donald Trump, has accused members of the administration of corruption.

Speaking on one of America's most popular podcasts, Mr Johnson said people around the president were catering to special interests. He based part of his criticism on allegations made by a former adviser in the White House.

The attack is striking because it comes from within the president's own party and from a senator who has generally stood by him. It follows a heavy defeat for Republicans in the midterm elections, which handed Democrats control of both the House and the Senate and set off a scramble inside the party over who and what was to blame.

Democrats have already promised wide ranging investigations of the administration once the new Congress sits in January. Criticism from Republicans such as Mr Johnson could make it harder for the White House to dismiss those inquiries as purely partisan.
