Father and teenage son among six Palestinians killed in fresh Israeli strikes on Gaza

10 Nov 2026, 15:49 GMT · London
Health sources in Gaza say at least six people died on Tuesday, adding to more than 1,600 deaths recorded since last year's truce.

At least six Palestinians were killed by Israeli fire across the Gaza Strip on Tuesday, including a man and his 13 year old son, according to local health sources.

Three of the dead were killed when an air strike hit a home belonging to the Ghubain family in the Daraj district of Gaza City. In the nearby Zeitoun neighbourhood, a separate strike on a tent killed the father and his son. A sixth person was shot dead by Israeli forces in Khan Younis in the south of the territory.

The deaths add to a toll that has kept rising despite the ceasefire brokered by the United States last year. Gaza's Ministry of Health says Israel has killed more than 1,600 Palestinians since the truce took effect, and that more than 74,000 people have been killed in the territory since October 2023.

The latest strikes came after the season's first heavy storm swept across Gaza at the weekend. The territory's government media office said around 70,000 tents were flooded within half a day, at least five people died, and damage was estimated at about $3m.

Officials in Gaza say Israel has allowed in only a small share of the shelters requested, admitting about 20,000 tents while holding back far larger numbers of tents and mobile homes. Roughly 85 per cent of homes in the strip have been destroyed or damaged, and aid deliveries remain restricted.

Talks on the next stage of the truce remain stuck. Negotiators have not agreed on how Hamas would give up its weapons or on a timetable for Israeli troops to pull back from the roughly two thirds of Gaza they still hold, areas from which residents have been told to leave.
