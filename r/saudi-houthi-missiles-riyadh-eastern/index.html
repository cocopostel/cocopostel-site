Saudi air defences down Houthi missiles fired at Riyadh and the Eastern Province

08 Nov 2026, 07:40 GMT · London
The Saudi led coalition says it destroyed several ballistic and cruise missiles aimed at the kingdom, as Houthi officials report dozens killed in coalition raids near Sanaa.

Saudi Arabia's air defences intercepted a fresh wave of Houthi missiles on Sunday, according to the Saudi led coalition fighting the group in Yemen. Coalition spokesman Major General Turki al Maliki said three ballistic missiles had been destroyed, two of them heading for Riyadh. A further ballistic missile aimed at the Eastern Province was also brought down, he said.

Later in the day the coalition reported that two cruise missiles had been intercepted over Riyadh and the south of the country. No damage or casualties inside the kingdom were reported.

The launches came hours after the Houthi run health ministry said coalition air raids on the village of al Shariyah, in the Bani Hushaish district east of Sanaa, had killed 33 people on Saturday. Its spokesman said eight women and 13 children were among the dead and at least 39 others were wounded, adding that homes, roads and rescue crews had been hit. The coalition did not immediately respond to the claim, and it could not be independently confirmed.

The Houthis' military spokesman claimed the coalition had carried out more than a hundred air and missile strikes across Sanaa and seven other provinces in a single day. He also said Houthi forces had mounted six attacks of their own on Aramco oil sites, Riyadh airport and Saudi military positions along the border.

The escalation follows a deadly strike on a market in Taiz province on Friday that killed 30 people, with each side blaming the other. Government forces launched a campaign last month to retake Houthi held territory, including the capital.

United Nations figures put the death toll at more than 1,600, with nearly 300,000 people displaced, since a 2022 truce broke down in July when the Houthis declared a blockade of Saudi ports.
