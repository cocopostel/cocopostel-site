Trump says Dana White press secretary offer was only a joke

09 Nov 2026, 01:10 GMT · London
A day after publicly urging the UFC chief to take the briefing room podium, the president told reporters he had never been serious.

Donald Trump has walked back his surprise offer to make Dana White, the head of the Ultimate Fighting Championship, the next White House press secretary, saying on Sunday that he had only been joking.

Talking to reporters as he returned to Washington from his Florida beach club, the president said he had simply enjoyed imagining his friend at the podium with half a dozen fighters standing behind him, reasoning that journalists would be far less inclined to give such a line up a hard time. He described the idea as a bit of fun.

The offer had been made publicly a day earlier, and it was never clear how seriously it was meant, although White had said he would travel to Washington to meet the president. The episode came as the White House reshuffles its senior staff after heavy Republican losses in the midterm elections.
