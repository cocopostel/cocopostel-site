Nvidia chief acknowledges relationship with a woman US officials call a Chinese spy

08 Nov 2026, 20:23 GMT · London
Jensen Huang told staff he had an extramarital relationship with a business executive whom American intelligence linked to Beijing.

Jensen Huang, the chief executive of the American chipmaker Nvidia, has acknowledged a personal relationship with a business executive whom United States officials concluded was working for Chinese intelligence.

Mr Huang confirmed an extramarital relationship in an internal message to employees on Sunday. The company has opened an investigation into the matter.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio had earlier warned officials about the entanglement. He played a part in drafting a secret memo on the subject, and senior figures in Washington were already concerned last spring.

American intelligence had cautioned that Mr Huang was involved with an executive recruited by China to gather information on the US company. The admission, and the inquiry that followed, add a sensitive national security question to the affairs of one of America's most valuable technology firms.
