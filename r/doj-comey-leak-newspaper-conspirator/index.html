Justice Department weighed naming a major newspaper as conspirator in Comey leak case

08 Nov 2026, 22:59 GMT · London
Prosecutors considered casting a national newspaper as a co conspirator in a leak case tied to James Comey, a step that would sharply escalate the administration's clash with the press.

The US Justice Department considered naming a major American newspaper as a conspirator in a leak investigation connected to former FBI director James Comey, according to a report published on Sunday.

It is not yet clear whether prosecutors will go ahead or how such a move would be framed. Treating a news organisation as a participant in a crime, rather than as the recipient of information, would mark a dramatic escalation in the Trump administration's campaign against the media and would be likely to trigger a serious legal fight over press freedom protections.

The disclosure lands as the department prepares for intense scrutiny from a Congress about to come under Democratic control after the midterm elections, with oversight of politically charged prosecutions expected to be high on the agenda.
