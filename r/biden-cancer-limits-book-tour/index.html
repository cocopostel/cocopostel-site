Biden says cancer will curb promotion of his new memoir

09 Nov 2026, 02:14 GMT · London
The former president said his aggressive prostate cancer is slowing him down and he will do fewer events for the book than he had hoped.

Former President Joe Biden has said his health will limit how much he can do to promote his forthcoming memoir.

Mr Biden, who is being treated for an aggressive form of prostate cancer, said the illness was slowing him down and that he would not be able to take part in as many conversations and appearances around the book as he had originally planned.

The former president, who left office in January 2025, disclosed his diagnosis last year.

The remarks offer a rare personal update from Mr Biden, who has largely kept out of public view, and come days after midterm elections in which his Democratic Party won control of both chambers of Congress.
