Rubio says further major combat against Iran is not needed

10 Nov 2026, 19:34 GMT · London
The secretary of state signalled Washington sees no requirement for new large scale military operations, echoing the president's talk of keeping the status quo.

Secretary of State Marco Rubio has said there is no need for further major combat operations against Iran.

His remarks follow President Donald Trump's comments a day earlier that he was keeping the status quo with Tehran and saw no need for new strikes, insisting that negotiations were making progress. The president has also described the war as effectively over after talks with Qatar's prime minister, who has been acting as a mediator.

Rubio did not set out what would happen if diplomacy stalled, and the comments do not mean the confrontation has ended. Tension remains high in the Gulf, where tankers have been struck near the Strait of Hormuz and Saudi Arabia continues to face missile and drone attacks from Yemen.

At home, pressure to wind down the conflict is growing. A new national poll found seven in ten Americans want Congress to pass a law ending the war, and Democrats taking control of Congress in January have made the issue a priority.
