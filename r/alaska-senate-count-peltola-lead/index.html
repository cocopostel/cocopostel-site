Peltola holds a narrow lead in Alaska as Trump insists Republicans won

08 Nov 2026, 15:00 GMT · London
The Democratic challenger is about 600 votes ahead of Senator Dan Sullivan with a quarter of ballots still to count.

President Donald Trump is continuing to claim victory for Republicans in Alaska's Senate race even though counting is far from over and the Democrat is narrowly in front.

Mary Peltola, the Democratic challenger, leads the Republican incumbent, Senator Dan Sullivan, by roughly 600 votes, according to tallies compiled by a US news agency. About a quarter of all ballots have still to be counted.

Alaska is known for slow counts, in part because of its vast distances and the time allowed for postal votes to arrive, so final results in close contests often take days or weeks.

The race is among a handful still too close to call after a midterm election in which Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress. A Peltola win would add to the scale of the Republican defeat and deepen questions over the president's hold on voters.

Until every remaining ballot is counted, neither side can claim the seat, whatever the president says.
