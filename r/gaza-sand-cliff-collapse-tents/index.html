Sand cliff collapses onto tents of displaced families in Gaza

08 Nov 2026, 21:05 GMT · London
At least five people, children among them, have been killed after a bank of sand gave way on a camp sheltering families forced from their homes.

At least five people have been killed in Gaza, among them children, after a sand cliff collapsed onto tents where displaced families had been sheltering.

Footage of the scene shows the aftermath, with makeshift shelters buried beneath the fallen sand as people search the site. The families living there had already been driven from their homes and were relying on tents for cover.

The deaths add to a grim run of days for Gaza's displaced population. The territory's first winter storm of the season has already flooded tens of thousands of tents, leaving many families without dry shelter.

The camps spread across Gaza offer little protection from the elements, and the collapse is a stark reminder of the risks faced by families with nowhere safer to go as winter sets in.
