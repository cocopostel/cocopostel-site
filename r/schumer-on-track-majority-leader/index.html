Schumer appears set to become Senate majority leader after Democratic sweep

07 Nov 2026, 16:22 GMT · London
Despite doubts in his own caucus and anger from some activists, aides say the Democratic leader has secured enough backing to take the top Senate job.

Chuck Schumer looks set to become majority leader of the US Senate after the Democrats' sweeping midterm victory, his aides say, despite lingering resistance inside his own party.

The New York senator has led Senate Democrats for years, but the election aftermath brought open questions about whether he should keep the post. Some colleagues have voiced scepticism about his leadership, and parts of the activist base have been hostile, arguing the party needs a fresh face to match the energy of its win.

Mr Schumer's team says he has now gathered enough pledges of support to be elected leader when the new Senate organises. If confirmed, he would control the chamber's agenda just as Democrats prepare to confront President Donald Trump with majorities in both houses of Congress.

The role will place him at the centre of coming fights over spending, presidential nominations and oversight of the administration. How he manages the divisions within his caucus may shape how effectively Democrats use their new power ahead of the 2028 elections.
