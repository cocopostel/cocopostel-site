Washington voters ban foie gras as chefs prepare to fight back

08 Nov 2026, 17:03 GMT · London
A ballot measure outlawing the force feeding of birds and the sale of foie gras passed easily in the US capital, helped by a campaign built around a duck.

Voters in Washington DC have approved a ban on foie gras, passing a ballot measure that outlaws the force feeding of birds and the sale of the delicacy in the city.

The proposal, known as Initiative 86, won comfortably at the polls on election day. Campaigners leaned heavily on a duck named Olivia, who became the friendly face of the effort and helped turn an animal welfare question into a popular cause.

The result is not the end of the matter. Restaurant owners and some well known chefs in the capital are getting ready to challenge the measure, setting up a fresh battle over the measure. Because Congress holds unusual authority over the laws of the District, the ban could also face scrutiny beyond the city before it takes full effect.
