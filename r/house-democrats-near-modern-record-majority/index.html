House Democrats close in on their biggest majority in modern times

10 Nov 2026, 20:00 GMT · London
With at least 254 seats already won and leaders forecasting nearly 260, the party is on course for a historic margin in the next House.

Democrats are heading for one of the largest House majorities in modern American politics as the final results of last week's midterm elections come in.

The party has now secured at least 254 seats in the 435 member chamber, and its leaders estimate the total could end up close to 260 once the remaining races are called. That would give Democrats a commanding cushion over Republicans when the new Congress meets in January and approach the high water mark for any party in recent decades.

The scale of the win reflects a national swing against the Republicans driven largely by frustration over the cost of living, with high fuel prices linked to the Iran war often cited, and unease about the direction of the Trump administration. Republicans, stung by their worst defeat in decades, have already moved to change their House leadership, choosing Jim Jordan as their leader in the minority.

A majority of this size would give Democrats room to pass legislation without relying on every member, and it strengthens their hand as they prepare investigations into the administration. It also sets up two years of divided government for the rest of President Donald Trump's term.
