Possible 2028 rivals to Vance start preparing after heavy Republican losses

08 Nov 2026, 10:00 GMT · London
The vice president has not decided on a White House run, but would be challengers are already moving after a bruising midterm night for the party.

Republicans who may want the party's presidential nomination in 2028 are beginning to position themselves after the party suffered painful defeats in the midterm elections.

Vice President JD Vance has long been seen as the natural heir to Donald Trump, but he has not yet made up his mind about running. His potential rivals, sensing that the scale of the losses has weakened any claim to automatic succession, are wasting little time in laying the groundwork for their own campaigns.

The results have prompted a wider debate inside the party about whether to stay loyal to the Trump brand or chart a different course. That argument is likely to shape an increasingly open contest, with several figures testing support among donors and activists well before any formal announcements.

Members of both parties have suggested that a crowded and competitive primary season could ultimately be healthy, forcing candidates and their ideas to face real scrutiny.
