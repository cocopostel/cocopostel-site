Settlers set Palestinian business ablaze in village near Nablus

10 Nov 2026, 23:33 GMT · London
A large fire engulfed Palestinian property in Luban ash Sharqiya in the occupied West Bank after an attack by Israeli settlers.

Israeli settlers have set fire to Palestinian property in Luban ash Sharqiya, a village near Nablus in the occupied West Bank, and a large blaze tore through a local business on Tuesday.

Images from the village showed the fire spreading across the site. There was no immediate word on whether anyone had been hurt or on the full cost of the damage, and no response from the Israeli authorities had been reported.

The attack adds to a long list of incidents across the West Bank in which settlers have targeted Palestinian homes, farmland, olive groves and livelihoods. Earlier this month settlers burned olive trees near Ramallah, and Israeli demolitions and raids have continued in towns and villages around Nablus.

Palestinians and rights groups have long accused Israel of failing to hold settlers to account for such violence, while the expansion of settlements in the territory has continued.
