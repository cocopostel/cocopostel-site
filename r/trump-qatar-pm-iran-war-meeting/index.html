Trump to host Qatar's prime minister as push to end the Iran war gathers pace

09 Nov 2026, 11:45 GMT · London
The White House meeting comes as efforts to stop the conflict intensify after the midterms, though officials on both sides warn a deal is not close.

Donald Trump is due to meet the prime minister of Qatar on Monday, as diplomacy aimed at ending the war with Iran picks up speed in the wake of last week's midterm elections.

Qatar has been one of the main go betweens in the conflict, and its leaders have kept channels open with Tehran throughout more than eight months of fighting. The talks at the White House are expected to focus on whether any path exists towards a lasting settlement.

Senior American and Gulf officials have nonetheless played down hopes of an imminent breakthrough. With no clear victory in sight, the president has suggested that a return to major combat is unlikely in the near future, but he has so far refused to accept a truce plan already put forward by Iran.

The meeting also comes as Trump grapples with the political fallout at home, having conceded over the weekend that parts of his immigration policy may have been too harsh. Allies in the region will be watching closely for any sign that the White House is ready to shift its position on the war.
