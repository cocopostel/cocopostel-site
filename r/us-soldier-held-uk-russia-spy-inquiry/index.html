American soldier detained in Britain on suspicion of spying for Russia

10 Nov 2026, 19:52 GMT · London
A 41 year old US Army member is in custody in England after a joint inquiry involving British police, the FBI and American military intelligence.

A serving member of the United States Army has been arrested in Britain on suspicion of spying for Russia.

The man, who is 41 and has not been publicly named, was being held in custody while investigators decided whether to bring charges. The arrest followed a joint investigation carried out by British counter terrorism police working alongside the FBI and American military intelligence.

The case is a rare instance of a US service member being detained on espionage suspicions on British soil, and it comes at a sensitive moment for transatlantic security. Washington is reviewing the size of its forces in Europe, and American aircraft have recently left British bases with little public explanation.

It also lands as American, Russian and Ukrainian officials hold talks on ending the war in Ukraine, with Western governments on alert for Russian intelligence activity across Europe.

No further details about the soldier's unit, his alleged contacts or the information he may have passed on have been released. Officials have not said when a charging decision is expected.
