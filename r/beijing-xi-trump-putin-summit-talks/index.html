Beijing confirms talks on a historic Xi Trump Putin meeting

07 Nov 2026, 03:12 GMT · London
China says a three way summit is under discussion while Ukraine faces fresh strikes on Kyiv and presses allies for Patriot missiles and postwar security guarantees.

China has confirmed that it is discussing a historic meeting between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin, a format that would place the Ukraine war and great power bargaining on the same stage.

Foreign ministry spokesperson Mao Ning said this week that such a gathering was under discussion. Analysts watching the track say Moscow would likely use any three leader summit to pull Beijing and Washington closer to Russian positions on Ukraine. Advisers around Trump have separately cautioned him against letting Ukraine dominate those talks, according to public briefings circulating after the Chinese remarks.

On the ground the war did not pause for summit talk. Ukraine again came under Russian attack on Saturday. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy repeated calls for allies to supply missiles for US made Patriot batteries to defend Ukrainian cities. Kyiv's mayor reported that an early Saturday missile strike damaged a residential building in the capital, part of weeks of near constant pressure on urban areas and energy sites.

Zelenskyy has also pressed Western partners for specific guarantees on how postwar security support would work once fighting with Russia ends. His comments followed two days of allied discussions in Brussels that brought together officials from the United States, Britain, France, Germany and the European Union, along with the World Bank and a major US investment group, according to Ukraine's lead negotiator.

The diplomatic calendar now holds two competing stories at once: a possible Xi Trump Putin encounter that could redefine leverage over any settlement, and a Ukrainian push for air defence munitions and written security promises before winter deepens the cost of damaged power and housing.
