Trump says Iran land blockade is in force as drone darkens Iraqi Kurdistan

09 Nov 2026, 00:00 GMT · London
Washington's squeeze on Iran's overland supply routes has begun, the president says, while a drone strike on a gas field leaves most of northern Iraq's Kurdish region without electricity.

President Donald Trump says the American economic blockade aimed at Iran's land supply routes, known as Iron Gate, is now in effect. The measure follows the creation of a task force charged with cutting off the overland corridors Tehran relies on for trade and supplies.

The president paired the announcement with a conditional offer. He said a return to American strikes on Iran was highly unlikely provided there was progress in negotiations over Tehran's nuclear programme and over reopening the Strait of Hormuz, the waterway whose closure has rattled energy markets throughout the war.

Across the border in Iraq, a drone struck the Khor Mor gas facility in the Kurdistan region, starting a fire and bringing production to a halt. The damage quickly rippled through the grid, and more than 70 per cent of the Kurdish region was left without electricity after the attack.

The outage adds to the strain on Iraq, which has already complained that outside actors are using its territory in the wider conflict, and it underlines how exposed regional energy infrastructure remains while talks between Washington and Tehran hang in the balance.
