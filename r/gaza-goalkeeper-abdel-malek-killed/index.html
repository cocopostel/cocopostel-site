Young Gaza goalkeeper killed days after a tournament honouring slain athletes

11 Nov 2026, 10:57 GMT · London
Mahmoud Abdel Malek, 20, who kept goal for Gaza Sports Club, died in an Israeli air strike on Gaza City.

A 20 year old goalkeeper for Gaza Sports Club has been killed in an Israeli air strike on Gaza City, only days after he took part in a football tournament held in memory of Palestinian sportsmen and women who have died in the war.

Mahmoud Abdel Malek had played in the competition, which commemorated 1,017 Palestinian athletes reported killed since October 2023. His death adds another name to that list.

The strike comes amid continuing Israeli attacks across the territory despite the ceasefire agreed in October 2025, and as displaced families battle flooding and winter storms in damaged tent camps. Sport in Gaza has been devastated by the conflict, with stadiums and training grounds destroyed and many clubs left without players, coaches or facilities.

No further details about the circumstances of the strike were immediately available.
