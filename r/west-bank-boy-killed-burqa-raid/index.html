Palestinian boy of 14 shot dead in Israeli raid near Nablus

10 Nov 2026, 11:23 GMT · London
Moataz Daghlas was killed in the village of Burqa, Palestinian officials say, as the Israeli army said troops fired at people preparing to throw stones.

A 14 year old Palestinian boy has been shot dead by Israeli forces during an operation in the occupied West Bank, according to Palestinian officials.

The Palestinian health ministry named him as Moataz Daghlas and said he was killed on Monday evening in Burqa, a village north west of Nablus. His family say the Israeli authorities are holding on to his body.

The Israeli military said its soldiers had spotted a group it described as terrorists preparing to throw stones in an area where Israeli vehicles had been targeted before, and that troops opened fire to remove the threat.

The Palestinian Red Crescent said its crews treated two people for gunshot wounds but were prevented from reaching a third injured person. It added that three of its volunteer medics were detained. The official Palestinian news agency said those hurt were a 17 year old, hit in the abdomen and pelvis, and a man of 19 who was shot in the hand.

United Nations figures show that at least 98 Palestinians, 22 of them children, have been killed by Israeli troops or settlers in the West Bank since January. Over the same period six Israelis, soldiers and civilians, have been killed there by Palestinians.

The army's rules on opening fire were reportedly loosened after the Hamas led attacks on 7 October 2023, giving soldiers more freedom to use deadly force when they feel threatened.

In June the UN human rights office for the occupied territory said it had confirmed the deaths of 241 Palestinian children at the hands of Israeli forces and settlers in the West Bank since that date. Most were teenage boys, and in many cases the Israeli side said they had been throwing stones. The office concluded that none of the children killed during confrontations had posed an imminent threat to life, which under international law is the only ground that justifies lethal force in the territory.
