Syria considers sending soldiers to back Saudi Arabia against the Houthis

10 Nov 2026, 16:43 GMT · London
Damascus has sent scouting teams to Saudi Arabia and Yemen as it weighs a possible deployment, though officials say no final decision has been taken.

Syria is weighing whether to send troops to support Saudi Arabia in its war with the Houthi movement in Yemen, according to officials familiar with the discussions.

Syrian reconnaissance teams have already travelled to both Saudi Arabia and Yemen to assess conditions ahead of any possible deployment. Officials stressed, however, that the government in Damascus has not yet made a final decision on whether its forces will take part.

The move, if it goes ahead, would mark a striking new role for Syria in the regional conflict and a sign of how far relations between Damascus and Riyadh have shifted. It would also widen the coalition confronting the Houthis at a time when Saudi territory has come under repeated missile and drone attack, including strikes aimed at Riyadh and at the kingdom's main east to west oil pipeline.

The war in Yemen has intensified in recent weeks, with heavy fighting on several fronts and rising civilian casualties. Any Syrian contribution would be watched closely by Iran, which has long backed the Houthis, and by Western governments seeking to contain the fighting.

No timetable for a decision has been given.
